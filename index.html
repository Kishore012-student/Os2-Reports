Traditional workflow

Where manual claim investigations stall

  • Manual transcription of scanned RC books, driver licenses, hospital bills, and police FIRs is slow and error-prone.
  • Fragmented evidence storage risks evidence repudiation and chain-of-custody violations in arbitration or court.
  • Subtle cross-document discrepancies — such as driver name mismatches or backdated hospital records — slip past manual reviews.
  • Drafting 10–20 page standardized Word and PDF investigation reports consumes hours per claim.
